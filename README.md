# ⚡ MONSTER JUICE - 3D OVERDRIVE EXPERIENCE ⚡

A high-voltage, cutting-edge promotional website for **Monster Juice**, supercharged with **Three.js 3D WebGL graphics**, **real-time procedural lightning storms**, **screen strobe flashes**, and **synthesized Web Audio**.

---

## 🌟 What's New: 3D & Flashing Features

1. **Real 3D WebGL Cylinder Can (Three.js)**:
   - True 3D aluminum beverage can with realistic bevels, top taper neck, silver rim, and pull tab.
   - **360° Drag & Spin**: Click/touch and drag the can to freely inspect it in full 3D space with inertia damping.
   - **"360° SPIN" Turbo Button**: Launches the can into a high-speed spin with strobe effects.
   - Procedural 1024x1024 high-res canvas texture wrap that re-renders dynamically on flavor switch.
   - Real-time metallic specular reflections, rim lighting, and orbiting 3D juice crystals.

2. **Electric Lightning Storm Engine**:
   - Fullscreen procedural branching lightning arcs that strike across the screen.
   - Dynamic electrical discharge sounds synthesized in real-time.

3. **Screen Strobe Flash & Camera Jolt System**:
   - High-voltage whiteout and neon shockwave screen strobe flashes when cracking the can, switching flavors, or claiming vouchers.
   - Screen shake animation simulating physical kinetic shockwaves.
   - **"⚡ STROBE FX" Toggle Switch** in the navbar to toggle flashing effects on or off.

4. **3D Holographic Cyber Chamber**:
   - Concentric 3D holographic rings orbiting the can in 3D perspective (`rotateX`, `rotateY`, `rotateZ`).
   - Glowing laser platform pedestal beneath the can.
   - Floating 3D badges with multi-layer depth.

5. **Multi-Plane 3D Interactive Cards**:
   - Gyroscopic 3D mouse parallax on cards and metrics with glowing cybernetic borders.
